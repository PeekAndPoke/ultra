## Review record (/feature-review catch-up gate, 2026-07-22, 3× Opus)

The realm shipped with coordinator self-review only; the full 3-agent gate ran 2026-07-22 against
HEAD (post `82601e91` AuthUser-seams refactor). All three reviewers confirmed the machinery sound
(single-use realm-scoped selection token, membership-before-grant, no IDOR/replay/cross-realm bleed,
plan permissions not tenant-influenceable) and converged on the org-lifecycle gap:

| Reviewer | Verdict | Key findings |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Impl & style | 2 HIGH | HIGH `OrgStatus` ignored by both hooks; HIGH e2e never drives `selectOrg` or its reject branches; MEDIUM no orgId dedupe (nondeterministic `firstOrNull` on duplicate memberships); MEDIUM weak assertions (no-org test passed on ANY non-OK) |
| 2. Domain | 1 HIGH | Same `OrgStatus` gap (suspension inert — refresh re-resolves unfiltered); MEDIUM JWT `accessibleOrgs` built from RAW memberships while the picker is vetted → deleted/suspended orgs still authorize `canAccessOrg()`; LOW `branchIds` trusted unvalidated |
| 3. Security | 1 MEDIUM | Same `OrgStatus` gap (abuse-containment bypass); LOW `createForSignup` `error()` → 500 if sign-up ever enabled; LOW fixtures seed org-ADMIN creds (rides fixtures-env-gate task); note: no server-side session revocation platform-wide (pre-existing) |

**All confirmed findings fixed (2026-07-22):**
- Both hooks enforce **active-only** (`OrgStatus.Active`): filtered in `getAccessibleOrgs`, rejected
  in `resolveSelectedOrg` (which also guards select-org + refresh → mid-session suspension bites on
  next refresh).
- `getAccessibleOrgs` dedupes by orgId; `resolveSelectedOrg` merges duplicate membership rows
  (union of roles/branchIds) — deterministic grants.
- `generateJwt` builds `accessibleOrgs` from the **vetted** set (memberships filtered to orgs the
  picker would show), closing the claim/picker divergence.
- `createForSignup` throws `AuthError.notSupported()` (fails closed as 403).
- `B2bAuthFlowTest` extended: selectOrg completion (Success + selected slug), single-use replay ⇒
  403, non-member orgId ⇒ 403, unknown orgId ⇒ 403, suspended-org matrix (only-org suspended ⇒ 403;
  one-active-left ⇒ auto-select), strict assertions (Forbidden + slugs, not just "not OK").

Deferred (recorded): branch-lifecycle validation of `branchIds` (LOW, latent until branch scoping
goes live); hoisting the active-only rule into shared machinery (module-dependency question:
funktor/auth must not depend on funktor/saas — revisit when b2b2c clones the hooks).
