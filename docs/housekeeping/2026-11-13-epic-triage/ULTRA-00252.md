## Decisions (numbered, with recommendations)

1. **00153 steps 7-8: delete `funktor/inspect/src/jsMain` (60 files) and port its sections to Vue?** Options: (a) do it now, one section at a time, cluster first; (b) park it under the Insights epic ULTRA-00253 and close 00153 as "mechanism built". Recommendation: (b). The mechanism is done; the port is feature work with its own review and deletion payoff, and it needs no other 00252 ticket. File one new ticket for step 7/8.
2. **00153 i18n and the `Message` key-vs-text wire change.** Options: build now, park into the i18n epic ULTRA-00256, drop. Recommendation: park into ULTRA-00256. Nothing is built, it changes the wire format, and the first Vue text beyond insights has not been needed yet.
3. **00162 SSE auth.** Options: (1) `streamHeaders` hook on `SdkConfig`; (2) `HttpTransport.stream()` so one wrapper covers both; (3) make `options` required. Recommendation: (2), since it is the only one that removes the silent asymmetry, plus (3) as a compile-time backstop. Security-critical: needs a red-team ticket. This is the highest-value open item.
4. **00194 / 00166 Vue binding: client via props, or `useClient()` / provide-inject?** Also 00168's realm question (bound at construction or per call). Recommendation: one composable accessor (provide/inject), pages never reach globals, realm per call stays (ticket's own recommendation). Decide once, then finish the 00166 binding layer and the 00194 test together.
5. **DOCS ticket for the SDK (00146 and 00153 follow-ups).** CLAUDE.md says no DOCS ticket for code still in flux. Recommendation: file it after D3 and D4 settle, one ticket covering `ultra/codegen`, `funktor/codegen`, profiles, contributors and the alias.
6. **00146 open finding: `@Slumber.Field` non-ctor props are emitted required.** Recommendation: leave, record as known limitation (they are response-only; no consumer reported).
7. **00146 open finding: `Char` emits bare `z.string()` (`TypeWalker.kt:300`).** Recommendation: leave (a refinement is a behaviour change for all consumers, no demand).
8. **00146: should `@SensitiveData` surface in emitted TS?** Recommendation: ignore, and write that into the ticket (the field is already on the wire). Closes the checkbox.
9. **00182 decision 1 (`Partial` degradation).** Recommendation: leave `Partial` as it is until a real consumer needs it; the change alters every client. Record and close.
10. **00168 / 00136:** revocation notice and matrix-on-refresh belong with ULTRA-00136 (auth epic ULTRA-00248). Recommendation: move the questions there and close 00168.
